# Day 15: Embeddings Basics

## What I Learned

I learned how text embeddings convert sentences into numerical vectors that represent their meaning.

I generated embeddings for 5 sentences and compared their semantic similarity using cosine similarity.

## What I Built
 
A simple embedding comparison project that:

1. Creates 5 sentences.
2. Generates embeddings for each sentence.
3. Calculates cosine similarity.
4. Compares the similarity between sentence pairs.

## Example

Two AI-related sentences:

- "I love learning artificial intelligence."
- "I enjoy studying AI and machine learning."

These sentences had a high similarity score because their meanings are closely related.

## Key Concepts

- Text Embeddings
- Vector Representations
- Semantic Similarity
- Cosine Similarity
